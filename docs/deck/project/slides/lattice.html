From quantiles to Markov price states

1 · States

Split each half-hour’s distribution into 11 equal-probability bins (normal-score space). State price = bin mean, from 16 sub-points of the inverse CDF; tails extrapolated.

2 · Transitions

AR(1) in normal-score space, Tauchen: z’ = ρ z + √(1−ρ²) ε. P(j | k) = Φ((e_j+1 − ρ n_k)/σ) − Φ((e_j − ρ n_k)/σ).

3 · Persistence ρ

Lag-1 correlation of past forecast errors (Gaussian copula on PIT values), refitted each month on earlier issues: about 0.74–0.78.

Why it matters

Independent stages let the optimiser plan to sell a spike and buy a dip an hour later, though real prices move together. Persistence removes those fake spreads; the expected value now sits below perfect foresight, as it must.

e_j are the bin edges in normal-score space, n_k the conditional mean of bin k. Rows are normalised. Equal-probability bins keep every state equally likely at each stage.
