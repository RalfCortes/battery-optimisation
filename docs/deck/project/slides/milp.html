The MILP: benchmarks and planners

One co-optimised problem

  • Charge, discharge, SoC and one binary per half-hour (never both)
  • Regulation, primary, contingency per half-hour, same NEMS rules as the simulator
  • Expected regulation energy in the SoC balance
  • End of window ≥ starting SoC; HiGHS, a year in about 5 s

Where it is used

  • Perfect foresight: realised prices, the upper bound
  • Self-schedule: median forecast, re-planned every half-hour, offered price-taking
  • Reserve planner (old): average forecast path, reserves handed to the SDP
  • Stochastic planner: reserves shared by 20 sampled paths, energy per path

Stochastic MILP: 1.1 s per issue with the binaries, 0.18 s relaxed (same objective in tests); a commercial solver would also help.

optim/milp.py · solve_schedule, solve_reserve_plan

The binaries are the whole cost of the stochastic plan: relaxing them is 6× faster. Only its reserves are used, never its energy schedule.
