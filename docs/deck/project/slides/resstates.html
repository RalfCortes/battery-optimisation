Change 4: reserve prices get their own trajectories

Before: one reserve price per stage

Energy had 11 Markov price states, but each reserve product had one point forecast per stage, the median. Reserve prices are skewed: realised means sit about 14% above the forecast median for every product.

Now: state = (energy k, reserve r)

One common reserve factor F, AR(1) with ρ = 0.75, in 5 equal-probability bins. Each product: zp = λpF + √(1−λp²)·ep; state price = E[pricep | F in bin r]. Revenue is linear, so the conditional mean is exact.
