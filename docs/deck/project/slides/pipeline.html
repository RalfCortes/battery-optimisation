Four layers, one simulator

1 · Forecast

Chronos-2

21 quantiles per half-hour for USEP and the three reserve prices. Calendar, regime, demand, ECMWF weather

2 · Prices

Markov states

11 price states per half-hour; prices persist (lag-1 correlation fitted walk-forward)

3 · Optimise

Water value

Stochastic DP over SoC × price state; energy and reserves chosen together

4 · Bid

Offer curves

Optimal response to price → 5 + 5 tranches; reserves offered beside it

Simulator: NEMS clearing, interval by interval

Price-taker clearing at realised prices → engine cuts the schedule to the real SoC (RC386) → battery physics, regulation energy, settlement. No look-ahead: offers fixed at gate closure.

Everything runs from typed configs (battery.yaml, market.yaml, bidding.yaml); 222 tests.
