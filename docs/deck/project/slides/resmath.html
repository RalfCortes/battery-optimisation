Change 3: reserves chosen inside the SDP

R_t(s→s’): a small LP per move

Maximise Σ value_p · x_p over x = (regulation, primary, contingency) subject to the NEMS rules at SoC s with the move’s charge c and discharge d:

  • d − c + reg + pri ≤ 100 and d − c + reg + con ≤ 100
  • (d/2 + reg/2 + pri/6 + con/3)/η − c·η/2 ≤ s − min, plus the 10-min and 30-min checks
  • (c/2 + reg/2)·η − d/(2η) ≤ max − s

Solved without an LP solver

The constraint matrix never changes; only the right-hand side does. Corners = every triple of active constraints, kept where all hold: computed once per SoC grid and cached (about 10 corners per move).

Per issue: one matrix product of corners × stage values, max over corners: 13 ms for 48 stages × 10,201 moves.

Value per MW held = forecast availability price × ½ h; regulation also counts its wear and the energy AGC moves (settled at the stage’s mean price, SoC loss valued the same).
