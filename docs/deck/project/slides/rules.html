Change 2: NEMS storage rules since 12 Aug 2025

RC383 · power

Limited per reserve class: energy + primary + regulation ≤ P, and energy + contingency + regulation ≤ P. Charging counts negative.

RC386 · SoC

The engine gets the real SoC 10 min ahead and cuts the schedule to what it can sustain: energy ½ h, regulation ½ h both ways, primary ⅙ h, contingency ⅓–½ h.

Settlement

Reserves paid for availability. Energy moved by regulation (AGC) or activations is ordinary energy at USEP. No pay if not delivered.

What changed in the simulator

Rules live in market.yaml (old pooled rules kept for comparison). The engine step keeps the highest-surplus tranches when the SoC binds. Regulation now moves energy: 5% up / 5% down of the MW held (assumption).

Sources: EMC rule changes RC383, RC386; Market Rules ch. 6, ch. 7, App. 6D

A contingency event is not a windfall: availability is what pays; activation energy is roughly break-even. 2025: 73 forced outages, 29 interruptible-load activations (0.2% of periods).
