#StepRule
1Validate offerPair counts, price order, limits, decimals; a bad offer stops the run
2Clear energyDischarge tranche clears if offer ≤ USEP; charge if bid ≥ USEP (price taker)
3Clear reservesTranche clears if offer ≤ reserve price; award capped at max_award_mw
4Engine SoC cutIf the schedule breaks a power or SoC rule at the real SoC: LP keeps the highest-surplus tranches
5Battery stepEfficiencies, SoC bounds; then regulation energy (5% up, 5% down)
6SettleUSEP × delivered energy + reserve price × MW × ½ h − wear − shortfall penalty

Shortfall penalty: 4,500 SGD/MWh, on by default as a safety net; zero today because the engine never schedules beyond the SoC.

Every interval is logged: price, flows, SoC, awards, revenue, wear, cut MW, regulation energy.
