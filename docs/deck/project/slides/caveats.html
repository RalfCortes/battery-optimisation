Caveats before trusting the totals

Reserve markets are small

Requirements: regulation 108 MW, primary ~175 MW, contingency ~411 MW. We allow 100 MW awards per product as a price taker. Total 2025 spend was 70 M SGD, and the backtest books ~30 M of it.

Regulation energy is assumed

5% up and 5% down of the regulation MW per interval; no public AGC data. In market.yaml, to model later.

Not modelled yet

Regulation charges and reserve cost share paid by the battery; reserve activation energy (rare, roughly break-even).

Noise

Yearly profit moves a few percent between runs on near-identical forecasts: differences under ~0.3 M are noise.

The reserve caps are the biggest open decision: they change absolute profits a lot and possibly which strategy wins.
