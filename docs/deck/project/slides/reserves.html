Why the SDP wins

It prices what each SoC level is worth for reserves and for trading, under the price uncertainty. Valuing reserves at their mean (+0.26 M) or with their own trajectories (+0.31 M) adds more.

Why the stochastic MILP loses

Each path’s energy sees that path’s future: arbitrage looks too valuable, so it holds 3.6 M fewer reserves and recovers only 2 M of energy. Fixing that needs a multi-stage tree, which is what the SDP is.
