The problem and how we score it

The asset

100 MW / 100 MWh battery, 85% round-trip efficiency, 5 SGD/MWh degradation cost

The market

NEMS real-time pool, 30-minute ex-ante prices, offers fixed 65 min ahead. Energy, regulation, primary and contingency reserve

The score

Profit in a clearing simulator on held-out data, as a share of perfect foresight; uplift vs a self-schedule baseline

Test year: 28 Oct 2025 – 27 Oct 2026, forecasts re-issued every half-hour (rolling 48 steps). Earlier years checked with daily forecasts.

Baselines: perfect foresight (MILP, same rules) · self-schedule (MILP plan on the median forecast, offered as price-taker)

The self-schedule always clears, so it is a price taker: a fair “no bidding skill” baseline, not a realistic strategy.
